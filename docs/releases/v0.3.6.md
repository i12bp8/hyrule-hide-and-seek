# Hyrule Hide & Seek v0.3.6

- A pulsing arrow around the hunter points along the precise bearing toward the prop. It
  rotates continuously as the hunter turns the camera or either player moves, including
  diagonal directions and props behind the hunter.
- Taunts, treasure pickups, optional stationary clues and hunter tracking use this feedback
  for three seconds. The clue card updates its direction and rough range alongside the arrow.
- Ordinary clues never place a marker on the prop. The final reveal keeps its separate marker
  at the reveal location and its existing timing.
- Arrows disappear when their clue expires or the hider is found, disconnected, off-stage or
  no longer sending current position updates. Tracking stays on the hider selected by the pulse.
- Keep the arrow clear of the status cards and control hints on small screens.

Compatible with **v0.3.5 rooms (protocol 10)**. Each hunter needs **v0.3.6** to see the new arrows.
Use **Dusklight 2.0.3 or newer**. Install the combined `hyrule_hide_and_seek.dusk` asset below
through the mod manager or your `mods` folder. It includes Linux, Windows, macOS, Android and iOS builds.

Validated with 4,982 automated checks and 452 live bearing checks in official Linux Dusklight
2.0.3, including clue expiry and removal of invalid targets. The arrow was inspected in-game;
other platforms still need runtime checks.
