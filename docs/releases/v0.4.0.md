# Hyrule Hide & Seek v0.4.0

Prop Hunt is now the only mode. Everyone in a room needs **v0.4.0 / protocol 11** and
**Dusklight 2.0.3 or newer**. Older rooms are incompatible; existing dedicated saves still work.

## Arenas and disguises

- 13 individually configured arenas with bounded play areas, standing spawns, themed palettes,
  extra native clutter and filtered story triggers. New interiors include Telma's Bar, Snowpeak
  Ruins and Arbiter's Grounds, alongside Bulblin Camp and Hyrule Castle Grounds.
- 67 selectable disguises, including 30 Castle Town citizens, animals, grasses, pumpkins,
  furniture and carryables. The invisible crystal disguise has been removed.
- Native light/shadow settings, idle/walk and material animations, grass sway, water-surface
  lily-pad bobbing and oil-jar secondary models. Round interaction prompts no longer expose real props.
- Solid props use native cylinders/meshes. Nearby meshes release distant collision slots and
  retry capacity failures instead of becoming permanently walk-through.

## Abilities and points

- Props: tap Up to place a decoy; hold Up for 0.45 s during Hunt to swap with the newest decoy,
  twice per round with a 15 s cooldown.
- Hunters: Up whistles every 40 s, making hidden props squeak at their positions. Down retains tracking.
- Equal 26-point round ceilings: 12 objective, 8 gross bonus and 6 win. The shared bonus allowance
  includes bold taunts, fooled hunters, close calls, first blood, quick finds and last standing.
- Ability/style cooldowns and limits survive late joins and host changes with different clocks.
  Duplicate/malformed snapshots do not partially apply scores or grant extra ability uses.
- Saved custom rules migrate; map selection resets to Random for the new arena catalogue.

## Crash fixes and checks

Link animations now read immutable archive bytes from the mod's host-backed heap. This removes
an extra mounted animation index table that exhausted the game's small System heap on entering
Telma's Bar. Archive bounds and animation entry indices are checked before use.

Validated on official Linux Dusklight 2.0.3: 5,704 rules checks, 16 relay/bot tests, 179 disc resources,
all 13 arena loads with repeated Telma's Bar visits, and the full-room renderer with all 67
disguises, 160 decoys, 24 treasure actors and constrained root-heap memory. See
[verification details](https://github.com/i12bp8/hyrule-hide-and-seek/blob/v0.4.0/docs/testing.md).
Other-platform runtime checks and paired human balance sessions remain on the device checklist.

The release bundle builds for Linux x86_64/aarch64, macOS x86_64/arm64, Windows amd64/arm64,
Android aarch64 and iOS arm64. Local lab/filming/test drivers are excluded.
