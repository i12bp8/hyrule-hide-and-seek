# Hyrule Hide & Seek v0.3.2

- Fix rupees immediately respawning at a collected location. Collected and expired spots keep
  a 1,200-unit exclusion for 30 seconds, and new rupees stay at least 600 units from active hiders.
  Other reachable areas continue to refill; standing on one spot cannot farm replacements.
- Preserve rupee respawn cooldowns when another player becomes host; reset them for a new round.
- Remove the separate nametag distance limits and four-tag cap. Tags follow loaded, visible
  player models at any range, while overlapping labels remain suppressed.
- Hide & Seek hider names now remain visible at long range. Prop Hunt still hides hider names
  from hunters.

Uses protocol 8 and Dusklight 2.0.3 or newer, compatible with v0.3.1 rooms. The host needs v0.3.2
for the rupee spawn fix; each player needs v0.3.2 for the nametag changes.
