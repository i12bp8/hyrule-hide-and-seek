# Hyrule Hide & Seek v0.4.1

Everyone in a room needs **v0.4.1 / protocol 12** and **Dusklight 2.0.3 or newer**.

## Whole maps

- No more invisible play-area fences. Every arena is the whole map the game loads with its spawn
  room; only loading exits are closed, and doors stay shut during a round.
- New scenery on every map: small themed groups of real pots, crates, barrels, pumpkins, rocks,
  skulls and furniture (452 objects in total). Groups stand against walls or in open ground,
  well apart, and never in a doorway, an exit, a narrow passage, shallow water or next to the
  spawn.

## Animals in every coat

- Every coat is its own disguise: white, black and brown cuccos, four cats and four dogs
  (75 disguises in total). Copying a real animal with D-pad right takes its exact coat.
- Fixed: cats, dogs and cuccos no longer cycle through all their coats every frame; their eyes
  blink like the real animals.

## Removed

- The hunter whistle is removed. D-pad up does nothing for hunters; tracking stays on D-pad down.

Validated on official Linux Dusklight 2.0.3: 5,847 rules checks, 16 relay/bot tests, 217 disc
resources, all 13 whole-map arena loads with their scenery, and the full-room renderer with all
75 disguises, 160 decoys and 24 treasure actors under constrained root-heap memory. See
[verification details](https://github.com/i12bp8/hyrule-hide-and-seek/blob/v0.4.1/docs/testing.md).
