# Hyrule Hide & Seek v0.3.3

- Misses now cost **half a heart** by default, including hits on decoys. Hosts can choose Off,
  quarter, half, three-quarter or one-heart penalties. The final quarter stays protected;
  exhausted misses add two seconds of recovery, and a confirmed find restores one heart.
- Replace repeated automatic reveals with **one final clue per surviving hider**, at 20 seconds
  remaining by default. Hosts can choose 0–60 seconds; 0 leaves voluntary clues only. Short
  hunts keep their first half quiet. Recent taunts or pickups delay the finale through the
  cooldown, and changing host does not repeat it.
- Stationary clues default to **Off**. A convincing hiding spot is safe; collecting rupees or
  taunting voluntarily trades a rough clue for points and extra-decoy funding.
- Every clue and tracking pulse gives only **Ahead / Behind / Left / Right** and
  **Near / In the area / Distant** for three seconds. No exact distances or world markers.
  Directions refer to the hunter's view when the clue arrives and stay fixed while turning
  or moving. Nearby hunters can still hear the positional taunt.
- Stop forcing hunters to draw their sword every half second. They can sheathe it to use
  Horse Grass and other map interactions, while the sword remains available for hunting.
- Update Rules, Guide, clue alerts and saved-rule migration. Previous automatic-clue Off
  choices stay Off; custom stationary timings, maps, times, decoys and other rules are retained.

Everyone in a room needs **v0.3.3 (protocol 9)** and **Dusklight 2.0.3 or newer**.
Older mod versions cannot join the same room. The relay needs no update.
