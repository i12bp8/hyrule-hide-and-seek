Remote Links animate again, arena exits keep the map loaded, and the default hunt is faster with
more help for hunters.

- Fixed the T-pose bug: valid Link BCK animations were rejected because their concrete type is 8.
  Other players now use their transmitted upper/lower animation blends, including sword swings.
- Added collision at loading-zone floors and authored exit volumes. Native loading transitions
  are blocked during a round; planned round warps still work.
- Every arena uses the free third-person field camera. Fixed-camera tags and compulsory story
  events cannot take over the round.
- Fixed retained collision pointers when arena actors are deleted during map transitions.
- Moved temporary arena streaming buffers out of the small system heap to avoid allocation
  failures when loading busy maps such as Castle Town.
- New defaults: **30 seconds hiding**, **180 seconds hunting**, **five hearts**, and a
  **quarter-heart** penalty for missed swings. The final quarter is protected.
- Taunts reveal a hider for **3 seconds**, with a **4-second cooldown**. Regular clues fire every
  30 seconds, every 20 on large maps, and every 10 in the last minute. Stationary taunts default
  to 20 seconds and work in both game modes.
- Hunters get a D-pad down tracking pulse: three seconds of direction and rough range to the
  nearest hider, with a 25-second cooldown. Hosts can disable it.
- Auto hunter counts adapt to map size. Random chooses compact maps for fewer than six players;
  large maps remain selectable. A recommended-rules button restores the balance.
- Old stock rules migrate automatically. Normal story saves regain their previous heart
  capacity/life when leaving a round.

Everyone in the room must update to **v0.2.0** (protocol 6). No relay upgrade is needed.
Download `hyrule_hide_and_seek.dusk` below and install it through Dusklight's mod manager or
replace the old bundle in your mods folder.

Validation: 2,590 rules/balance/collision checks (also under AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer),
15 relay/bot tests, Linux GCC/Clang builds, and a stock Dusklight sweep of all 15 maps. The retained
rendering stress test covers a 16-player lobby, 50 prop kinds and 160 decoys. Manual cross-platform
multiplayer and exploration of every pathway remain part of the
[in-game checklist](https://github.com/i12bp8/hyrule-hide-and-seek/blob/v0.2.0/docs/testing.md).
