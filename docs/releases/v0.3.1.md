# Hyrule Hide & Seek v0.3.1

All players need protocol 8 and Dusklight 2.0.3 or newer. Protocol 7 rooms are incompatible.

- Treasure explores connected ground during hiding time and spreads across distant regions;
  up to 24 rupees, at least 1,200 units apart, each lasting 90 seconds.
- Control hints start on for new and upgraded installations. Turning them off afterwards is saved.
- Both starting roles have a 24-point ceiling: 12 objective, 6 bonus and 6 win points. Hunters
  share capture progress, and personal finds award bonus points. Hider survival scales with the
  chosen hunt duration, while loot/taunts have a shared gross bonus limit that spending cannot reset.
- Rupees give 1 point (2 in Treasure Rush), with up to 2 more at the third pickup.
- Recommended stationary clues use 30 seconds; final-minute clues use 15 seconds, or 12 for
  the last hider. Existing stock settings migrate; custom timing/clue profiles remain.

See [balance references and playtest guidance](https://github.com/i12bp8/hyrule-hide-and-seek/blob/v0.3.1/docs/balance.md). Real-map pickup reachability,
runtime performance and win rates still need paired in-game checks.
