# Hyrule Hide & Seek v0.3.5

- Hunters now lose the round's hearts all the way to zero. An eliminated hunter watches until
  the next round and cannot tag props. Hiders win immediately when every hunter is out.
- Only a confirmed find restores one heart, capped at five. Spring water, heart pickups,
  fairies and other world healing cannot refill hunters. New rounds and infected hunters
  start with five hearts. Half-heart miss penalties remain adjustable from Off to one heart.
- Keep hunter life and acknowledged misses on the host. Duplicate reports cannot charge twice;
  pending misses and eliminations survive host changes. Combo cuts cannot keep postponing a miss.
- Add direction arrows to taunts, treasure clues and hunter tracking, with rough range bands.
  Ordinary clues last three seconds and reveal a broad direction captured when the clue arrives.
- Show each surviving hider's precise location with a world marker for three seconds, once per
  round at 20 seconds remaining by default. Hosts can adjust that time from 0–60 seconds or turn
  it Off. Short hunts keep the first half quiet. Markers stay at the reveal spot so props can escape.
- A recent taunt or pickup cannot delay, erase or extend the final reveal. Hiders see a distinct
  LOCATION REVEALED alert. Quiet hiding stays safe before the finale with the recommended rules.
- Fix the Windows build of the new clue alert. Hunters can still sheathe their swords normally
  to use Horse Grass, call Epona and interact with the map.

Everyone in a room needs **v0.3.5 (protocol 10)** and **Dusklight 2.0.3 or newer**.
Install the combined `hyrule_hide_and_seek.dusk` asset below through the mod manager or your
`mods` folder. It includes Linux, Windows, macOS, Android and iOS builds.
