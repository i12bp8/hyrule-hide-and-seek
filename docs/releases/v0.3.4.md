# Hyrule Hide & Seek v0.3.4

- Add visible direction arrows to taunts, treasure clues and hunter tracking. The arrow matches
  the broad Ahead / Behind / Left / Right clue and keeps the rough distance band. Ordinary
  clues still last three seconds and give no precise world marker.
- The single final reveal now shows each surviving hider's **precise location with a world
  marker for three seconds**, at **20 seconds remaining** by default. Hosts can adjust the
  time from 0–60 seconds or turn it Off. Short hunts keep the first half quiet.
- Fire the final marker on time even after a recent taunt or rupee pickup. It stays at the
  reveal location, so hiders can escape afterward. Subsequent pickups cannot erase or extend it.
- Show hiders a distinct **LOCATION REVEALED** alert during the exact final reveal. Markers
  disappear when the hider is found, leaves the map or the round ends.

Half-heart miss penalties and the other v0.3.3 balance rules are unchanged.
Uses **protocol 9** and **Dusklight 2.0.3 or newer**, compatible with v0.3.3 rooms.
Use v0.3.4 on the host for the precise timing and on hunters for the new arrows and markers.
